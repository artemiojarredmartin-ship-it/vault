## Getting people to come back (no casino tricks)
1. **The Daily Drop** — once a day at a random time, the whole league gets the same surprise 2-minute challenge (BeReal-style).
2. **League streak** — shared flame that grows each day everyone proves everything; one miss breaks it for all.
3. **9pm last call** — push alert: "Read 20 pages still open — you're 5 chips behind Dre." (needs push notifications)
4. **Sunday awards** — weekly MVP / Most Sus / Comeback / Slacker as shareable cards.
5. **Double-chip day** — one surprise day a week counts double for everyone.
6. **Season Wrapped** — shareable end-of-season recap (streaks, best proof photo, the consequence video).
7. **Underdog shield** — last place gets one free miss per week so they don't quit.
8. **Chip skins** — unlock chip designs by winning seasons (status only).

Avoid: anything you can buy, loot boxes, fake countdowns.
